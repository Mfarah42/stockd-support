Privacy · Stockd for iOS

Your lists are yours.

Stockd ("the App") is developed by SimplyMaf. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how we handle your information — which is, mostly, that we never see it.

Last updated: April 9, 2026

Data collection

Stockd does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All your grocery lists, preferences, and app data are stored locally on your device and are never sent to any server.

Third-party services

Stockd does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. No data is shared with third parties.

Data storage

All app data is stored locally on your device using on-device storage. If you delete the app, all associated data is permanently removed.

Children's privacy

Stockd does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. The app does not require any personal information to function.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at stockdapphelp@gmail.com.
