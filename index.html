FAQ

Frequently asked

The short version of how Stockd handles your lists and your data.

Data

Does Stockd collect my data?

No. Stockd does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Your grocery lists, preferences, and app data stay on your device and are never sent to any server.

Read the privacy policy →
AI

Where does the AI run?

On your iPhone. List generation uses Apple's on-device Foundation Models, so saying "taco night for 6" builds the whole list without an account, an API, or a network call. It works in airplane mode.

Storage

What happens if I delete the app?

All app data is stored locally on your device. If you delete the app, all associated data is permanently removed with it — there is no copy anywhere else.
